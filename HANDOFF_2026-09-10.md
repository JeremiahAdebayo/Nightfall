## 4. Flow (canonical, post-restructure)

1. **Train** `scripts/train.py` → `nightfall.data.mvtec.ensure_mvtec_downloaded()`
   (anomalib datamodule, HF `TheoM55/mvtec_all_objects_split` fallback) →
   `PatchCore.fit_from_paths()` → WRN50 layer2/3 hooks → 3×3 local pool →
   multi-scale concat → greedy k-center 1% coreset (JL-projected distances) →
   per-category bank → `{cat}_memory_bank.pt` + `training_manifest.json`
   (resume-safe: checkpoint **and** manifest must both say complete).
2. **Eval** `scripts/run_eval.py` → `eval/dataloader.py` (index-paired masks) →
   `EvalHarness` → image/pixel AUROC + PRO (integrated to FPR≤0.3).
3. **Calibrate** `scripts/calibrate_thresholds.py` → mean+3σ of **training**
   scores → `thresholds.json`.
4. **Serve** `run_grpc_server.py` (banks + thresholds loaded once; refuses a bank
   with no threshold) → `rest_gateway.py` (grpc.aio, one reused channel, per-call
   deadline) → ESP32 → localtunnel plain HTTP.
5. **Quantize** `export_onnx.py` → `quantize_onnx.py` → `run_eval_int8.py`
   (mismatched-bank baseline) → `benchmark_latency.py` (CPU-only, deliberately).

**Invariant that took two diagnosed failures to learn:** the extractor that builds
a bank and the extractor that scores against it must be the same. fp32-bank +
INT8-features collapsed categories to near-chance; refitting with the INT8
extractor restored them (hazelnut 0.486→1.000, metal_nut 0.623→0.998).

## 5. Results (from the notebook, not reproducible here)

fp32 mean image AUROC **0.938**, PRO **0.651** over 15 categories (per-category
table in README). Paper reference mean 0.991/0.981/0.935; the ~5pt gap is
explained, not hidden. INT8: 99.6MB→25.0MB (3.98×), bottle 0.894 with
reweighting disabled, and **slower on CPU** (p50 1304ms vs 293ms) because the
Colab AMD EPYC 7B12 has AVX2 but **no VNNI**. Cable/toothbrush/transistor have
strong AUROC but weak PRO — detection without localization.

   a drift risk I left alone (single-source-of-truth for *results* is a bigger

   matches `test/x/001.png` ↔ `ground_truth/x/001_mask.png` by convention and
   its docstring says a silent mismatch would corrupt every metric. Still true.
5. **`torch.load` has no `weights_only=True`/`map_location`** anywhere. Harmless
   for your own checkpoints; add before anything untrusted touches the server.
6. **No CI.** `pytest` exists and is fast (CPU, no dataset); nothing runs it.

   `C:\Users\Jeremiah\scripts\noctis-swebench\noctis-swe\Scripts\python.exe` —
   *another project's* venv. Nightfall has no venv of its own. Fix:
   `python -m venv .venv && .\.venv\Scripts\pip install -e ".[dev,data,serving,quantize]"`.
8. `AGENTS.md` in this root belongs to `noctis-swebench` (mentions it by name and
   Qwen harness rules), is untracked, and was left alone. Either it's misplaced or
